# Company Intelligence — Intelligence directory data contract

This is the acceptance contract for the six child routes in the `Intelligence`
directory. It is a data/interface contract for the renderer; it is not a second
dashboard and it does not authorize browser-side inference.

## Shared rules

- The authoritative input is the selected company row in
  `Henneth Desk 2.CI.0/data/company_intelligence.json`, produced by
  `scripts/build_ci_slice.py`. A renderer may present or link emitted values,
  but must not derive a new financial claim, status, peer set, probability, or
  causal effect in the browser.
- Every factual row must retain a source reference: preferably `source_url` plus
  `document_id` and `page`; event/graph/case objects may use their nested
  `evidence` object when that is the emitted contract. Missing provenance is
  displayed as unavailable, never filled from a sibling route.
- Empty means no retained objects were emitted. Blocked means a deterministic
  gate deliberately withheld an output. Both states need plain-language copy;
  machine codes belong only in an explicitly collapsed technical disclosure.
- `research` is the directory hub, not a second source of intelligence. Its
  cards must route to the specialist views below and must not duplicate their
  data or semantics.

## Duplicate / legacy semantics to remove during migration

- Do not add a second `research` payload or a second graph/timeline builder in
  the directory landing. The existing specialist renderers are the seams.
- `timeline` is explicitly legacy in the current tree label. Keep it as the
  typed chronology acceptance target until the directory migration is complete,
  then remove the duplicate route only after its retained chronology is folded
  into the chosen Intelligence landing contract and its callers/checks are
  removed together.
- Existing `changes`, `sources`, `brief`, `thesis`, `watchlist`, `monitoring`,
  `conditional`, and `causal` tools remain specialist routes elsewhere; do not
  copy their cards into `research` or silently reclassify them as Intelligence
  child data.
- `event_studies` are descriptive historical benchmarks, not causal evidence or
  forecasts. They belong to Operating Intelligence and must not be presented as
  an implicit `graph` or `timeline` signal.
